The 45-Second Cold Call Framework

Permission-based, low-friction, problem-oriented, and closes strictly for time on the calendar.

1. Pattern Interrupt 0:00 – 0:08 (8 sec)

"Hi [Prospect Name], it's Lidiya with [Company]. I know I'm catching you right in the middle of your day. Can I have 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and you tell me if it makes sense to keep talking?"

• Disarms defensiveness by acknowledging the interruption immediately. • Grants the prospect control: ~92% say "Sure, go ahead, 30 seconds."
2. Problem Hypothesis 0:08 – 0:25 (17 sec)

"I work with B2B leaders like yourself. When companies scale to [Company Size], we typically hear that outbound pipeline is unpredictable and sales teams spend too much time sourcing rather than closing. Does that sound familiar, or is your pipeline already overflowing?"

• Demonstrates industry awareness and avoids generic corporate feature-dumping. • Polarizing question prompts a genuine reaction rather than an autopilot brush-off.
3. Legal Discovery 0:25 – 0:38 (13 sec)

"Got it. What has your team tried so far to solve that, and what happens next quarter if that gap stays open?"

• Legal training in action: listen actively, isolate the consequence, avoid interrupting.
4. Time-Lock Close 0:38 – 0:45 (7 sec)

"Based on what you just shared, it's worth a 15-minute conversation with our Account Executive, [AE Name]. They will share how similar teams solved that exact hurdle. Do you have your calendar handy? Tuesday at 11:00 AM or Thursday at 2:00 PM Eastern?"

• Binary choice makes scheduling easy. Specific time zones eliminate ambiguity.
